This repository provides basic information to help visitors understand how public transport works in Dubai, UAE. It is intended for tourists and first-time users who want simple guidance on using the Metro, buses, trams, and Nol cards.

Dubai has a modern and efficient transport system, but many travelers are unsure about fares, card usage, and balance checks. This guide summarizes the essentials in one place.

🚇 What is a Nol Card?

A Nol card is a smart travel card used to pay for:

Dubai Metro

Public buses

Dubai Tram

Water buses

Some parking services

You tap the card at entry and exit gates, and the fare is automatically deducted based on travel zones.

🔎 Checking Nol Card Balance

Travelers often need to confirm their balance before starting a trip to avoid issues at metro gates.

You can check it at station machines, ticket offices, or online using the card number. A simple step-by-step explanation of the online method is available here:
([Nol Card Balance Check Guide](https://mynolbalance.com/check-nol-card-balance-with-number/)) 

🚦 Tips for Tourists Using Dubai Transport

Keep enough balance for longer trips across zones

Avoid peak hours for a more comfortable ride

Follow carriage rules (Gold Class & Women/Children cabins)

Use route planning apps like Google Maps or RTA tools
