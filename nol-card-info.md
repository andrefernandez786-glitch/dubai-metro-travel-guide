Nol Card Basics for Tourists

The Nol card is a rechargeable smart card used for public transport payments in Dubai.

Where You Can Use It

Dubai Metro

Public buses

Dubai Tram

Some water transport

How It Works

You tap your card at the entry and exit gates. The system calculates your fare based on zones traveled.

Important Things to Remember

Keep the card with you during the entire journey

Do not forget to tap out

Maintain minimum balance to avoid access issues

Balance Checking

Tourists often forget to verify their balance before trips. You can check balance at ticket machines, bus stations, or through online methods before heading to the metro.
