Title: Dubai Metro Tips for Visitors

Dubai Metro is one of the easiest ways for tourists to travel around the city. Here are practical tips to make your journey smoother:

1. Buy a Nol Card

You cannot use cash on the Metro. Purchase a Nol card at the airport or any station and keep it topped up.

2. Check Your Balance Before Travel

Always make sure your Nol card has enough balance. If the balance is too low, metro gates will not open and you may be delayed. You can check at machines or online before leaving.

3. Understand the Cabin Types

Regular cabins are for everyone

Gold Class cabins require a Gold Nol card

Women & Children cabins are reserved

Using the wrong cabin can result in fines.

4. Travel Outside Peak Hours

Morning and evening rush hours can be crowded. Midday travel is more comfortable.

5. Follow Station Signs

All stations have clear English and Arabic signs, making navigation easy even for first-time visitors.
